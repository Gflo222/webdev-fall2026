CORNED BEEF HASH RECIPE

description

Corned beef hash presents itself as an exalted gastronomic tapestry, wherein sumptuous morsels of meticulously cured and brined beef are ceremoniously entwined with luxuriant cubes of golden potato, each possessing a delicately bronzed exterior that yields beneath the slightest pressure to reveal a yielding, almost silken interior. Interspersed throughout this savory mosaic are onions, transmuted through patient caramelization into translucent fragments of profound sweetness, while the entire composition is perfumed with an intricate constellation of herbs, spices, and the ineffable richness of rendered fat. The result is an unapologetically rustic yet aristocratically indulgent tableau—a magnificent convergence of salt, savor, sweetness, texture, and warmth that elevates humble pantry provisions into an object worthy of gastronomic reverence.

ingredients

  • Corned beef
  • Potatoes
  • Yellow onion
  • Butter or cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Garlic
  • Bell peppers
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • Paprika
  • Fresh parsley
  • Eggs, typically served on top of the hash

instructions

  1. Prepare the ingredients. Dice 2 cups of cooked corned beef, 3 medium potatoes, 1 medium yellow onion, and ½ bell pepper. Mince 2 cloves of garlic. Chop a small handful of fresh parsley.
  2. Cook the potatoes. Place the diced potatoes in a pot of salted water. Bring to a boil and cook for approximately 5–7 minutes, until they are just tender but not falling apart. Drain thoroughly.
  3. Sauté the vegetables. Heat 2 tablespoons of butter or cooking oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and bell pepper and cook for 4–5 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic and cook for another 30 seconds.
  4. Add the potatoes. Spread the potatoes evenly across the skillet. Season with ½ teaspoon salt, ½ teaspoon black pepper, and ½ teaspoon paprika. Cook for 8–10 minutes, turning occasionally, until the potatoes develop a deep golden-brown crust.
  5. Add the corned beef. Add the diced corned beef and mix everything together. Cook for another 5–7 minutes, allowing the beef to brown and develop crisp edges.
  6. Add additional seasoning. Stir in 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce and cook for approximately 1 minute so it coats the mixture evenly.
  7. Allow it to crisp. Press the hash into an even layer in the skillet. Leave it undisturbed for 3–4 minutes so the bottom becomes crisp. Flip or stir, then repeat once more.
  8. Finish the dish. Remove from the heat and sprinkle with the chopped fresh parsley.
  9. Prepare the eggs. In a separate pan, fry 2–4 eggs to your preferred level of doneness. Place the eggs over individual portions of the hash.
  10. Serve immediately. The finished hash should have crisp, deeply browned potatoes and corned beef on the exterior while remaining tender and savory throughout.
