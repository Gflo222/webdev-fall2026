MAC AND CHEESE RECIPE

Description

An indulgent symphony of delicate pasta ribbons, luxuriantly cloaked in a silken, golden cheese velouté of unparalleled richness. Each perfectly tender morsel is harmoniously infused with the nuanced essence of artisanal fromage, creating a transcendent marriage of texture, aroma, and flavor. A humble culinary classic, exquisitely reimagined as an experience worthy of the finest gastronomic salons.

Ingredients

  • 6 oz small shell pasta or elbow macaroni
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • 1½ cups freshly shredded sharp white cheddar
  • ¼ cup freshly shredded mild cheddar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp onion powder
  • ⅛ tsp garlic powder
  • ⅛ tsp paprika
  • ⅛ tsp black pepper
  • 4 tbsp pasta water, reserved

Instructions

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta until just tender, about 8–10 minutes. Before draining, reserve ½ cup of pasta water.
  2. Melt the 2 tbsp butter over medium-low heat. Whisk in the 2 tbsp flour and cook for about 1 minute.
  3. Slowly whisk in the 1 cup milk and ½ cup heavy cream. Keep whisking until the sauce thickens slightly, about 3–5 minutes.
  4. Reduce the heat to low. Add the cheddar a handful at a time, whisking until completely melted before adding more.
  5. Stir in the salt, onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, and pepper.
  6. Add the drained pasta and stir until every shell is luxuriously coated. If it's too thick, add 1 tbsp pasta water at a time until you reach that silky boxed-mac consistency.
  7. Taste and adjust the salt. Serve immediately.
