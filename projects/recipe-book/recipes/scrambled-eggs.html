SCRAMBLED EGGS RECIPE

description

Scrambled eggs constitute an ostensibly modest yet profoundly sophisticated gastronomic preparation, wherein fresh eggs are meticulously amalgamated into a seamless, lustrous emulsion before being subjected to the restrained influence of heat. Through deliberate and continuous agitation, the liquid egg gradually relinquishes its former fluidity, coalescing into sumptuous, golden curds of remarkable delicacy while preserving an interior of exceptional tenderness and moisture. The restrained incorporation of butter contributes a silken richness and subtle depth, while judicious seasoning serves to accentuate the eggs' inherent savoriness rather than overwhelm it. The resulting composition is a study in culinary restraint: an elegant convergence of richness, texture, warmth, and simplicity, elevated through nothing more than precision, patience, and impeccable technique.

ingredients

  • egg
  • butter
  • salt
  • pepper

instructions

  1. Crack 3 eggs into a bowl.
  2. Add ¼ teaspoon salt and ⅛ teaspoon black pepper.
  3. Whisk the eggs thoroughly until the yolks and whites are completely combined and the mixture looks smooth.
  4. Place a nonstick skillet over low to medium-low heat.
  5. >Add 1 tablespoon of butter and allow it to melt completely without letting it brown.
  6. Pour the eggs into the skillet.
  7. Let them sit undisturbed for about 10–15 seconds, then gently stir with a spatula.
  8. Continue slowly stirring and folding the eggs every few seconds. Scrape the bottom and sides of the pan as you go.
  9. Cook for approximately 2–3 minutes, until the eggs form soft, moist curds. Do not wait until they look completely dry; they will continue cooking from residual heat.
  10. Remove the eggs from the heat immediately and transfer them to a plate.
  11. Serve immediately while they are still soft and creamy.
