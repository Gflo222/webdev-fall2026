Made by Griffin Florsheim and Teagan Druger

The REAL Real Estate is a distinguished purveyor of extraordinary residences, dedicated to transforming the boundaries of conventional architecture into destinations of unparalleled character and imagination. Far removed from the monotonous rows of ordinary houses, our collection encompasses remarkable dwellings conceived in the forms of bananas, potatoes, boots, and countless other wonderfully unconventional designs. Each property is more than merely a place to reside; it is an architectural statement, a conversation piece, and an experience in its own right. With an uncompromising appreciation for originality, craftsmanship, and the delightfully absurd, The REAL Real Estate specializes in procuring and presenting homes that refuse to be forgotten. Whether one dreams of residing within a colossal culinary creation, inhabiting an architectural curiosity, or simply possessing the most unmistakable address in the neighborhood, our company exists to make the extraordinary not merely imaginable, but inhabitable.
